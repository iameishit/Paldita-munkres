# Governance

munkres is a small project with a maintainer-led model.

- **Decisions** are made by the maintainers after discussion in issues and pull requests.
  Disagreements are resolved by the maintainers, favouring correctness, backwards
  compatibility and a small, dependency-free core.
- **Changes** go through pull requests that pass the full check suite
  (`tools/audit.sh`, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **Releases** follow [release/VERSIONING.md](release/VERSIONING.md) and
  [release/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md](release/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md).
- **New maintainers** are invited by existing maintainers after a sustained record of
  high-quality contributions and reviews.
- **Conduct** is governed by the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
