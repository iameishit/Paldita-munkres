# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(licensed CC BY 4.0). In short:

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory jokes.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do
not follow this code, and may ban contributors for behaviour they judge harmful. To report
a problem, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile
([@iameishit](https://github.com/iameishit)). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
